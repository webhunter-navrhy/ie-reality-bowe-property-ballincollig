About the property

Description property

This charming and quaint two-bedroom mid-terrace home is ideally positioned in Cork City and offers a wonderful blend of character, comfort and future potential. Dating from circa 1900 and extending to approximately 83 sq. m., the property has been exceptionally well maintained throughout and presents in excellent condition. Retaining much of the charm associated with a home of its era, it has also been thoughtfully updated over the years to provide comfortable modern living. The accommodation is well proportioned and offers a warm and inviting feel throughout, with the recently upgraded kitchen being a particular highlight. The two bedrooms are well suited to a variety of needs. One of the property's standout features is undoubtedly the exceptionally large rear garden. Enjoying a wonderful westerly aspect, the garden offers an abundance of outdoor space and is a rare find for a city property of this type. Located within Cork City, the property is ideally placed for those looking to enjoy the convenience and vibrancy of city living, with a wide range of amenities, shops, cafés, restaurants, schools and transport links all within easy reach. This is a delightful home that combines period charm with excellent presentation and an exceptionally generous rear garden.
